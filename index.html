Welcome

A private, on-device log for tracking sexual encounters, partners, and testing history - built for your own records and for conversations with medical professionals.
Nothing leaves this device. Everything you enter is stored locally in this browser only - never uploaded, synced, or tied to any account.
What you can do here
  • Log entries per partner, with as much or as little detail as you're comfortable with
  • Track testing history, results, and treatment over time
  • Export an anonymized report for a doctor, or a full backup for your own records
Next, we'll ask a couple of quick questions about you - this just decides which sections of the entry form are relevant, and you can change any of it later.

Quick Tour

Log Entries

Every encounter gets its own entry - date, partner, and as much detail as you're comfortable recording. Fields left blank are simply marked unspecified.

Same-Day Ordering

Logged more than one entry on the same date? Small arrows like these show up on those entries so you can reorder them to match what actually happened, without ever needing to touch the date.

Your Profile

This is where your own info lives - it decides which sections show up on the entry form. You'll set the basics up right after this tour, and can come back and change any of it whenever you like.

Partners Get an ID

Each partner you save is assigned its own ID, like #0007, that stays with them across every entry - so you never have to retype their details twice, and multiple entries for the same person can be linked together in an anonymized export for a doctor without ever naming them.

Track Testing

Log test dates, symptoms, results, and treatment history in one place, so it's easy to see where things stand over time.

Export How You Need

Anonymized is selected by default - ready to share with a doctor. Switch to Full for a complete record with names and usernames included, and narrow to just a search result or a single partner's history any time.

Deleted Entries

Deleted an entry by mistake? It's kept here for 7 days before it's gone for good, so there's a safety net if you tap the wrong thing.

Settings

The gear icon holds the housekeeping: a full backup report, exporting or importing your raw data, deleting everything, and - if you ever want to see this tour again - restarting it from here.

Everything Stays Here

Nothing is uploaded or synced. Your data lives only in this browser, on this device, and you're always the one in control of it.

One Last Step: Your Profile

Right after this, you'll be asked to set up your profile. It only decides which sections show up on the entry form, and takes a few seconds:

Required - under Genitalia, tick at least one of Penis or Vagina (tick both if that applies to you). That's the only thing you actually need to pick.

Everything else on that screen - gender identity, PrEP/PEP status, vaccines, allergies - is optional. Skip anything you don't want to fill in now; you can add or change it later from the Profile button up top.
